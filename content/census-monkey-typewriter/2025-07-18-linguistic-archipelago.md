## The Paradox of Linguistic Islands

In 2019, American counties with linguistic isolation—places where a non-English language dominated far more than anywhere else in their state—challenge conventional wisdom about economic integration. Rather than struggling economically, these isolated communities often demonstrate remarkable economic resilience.

[View full analysis →](/census-monkey-typewriter/analyses/linguistic-archipelago.html)

### Key Findings

- Counties with high linguistic isolation show unexpected economic mobility patterns
- Geographic clustering of language use creates economic advantages rather than barriers
- Border regions and established ethnic enclaves demonstrate distinct success patterns

- US Census Bureau American Community Survey (2019)
