## The Great Housing Mismatch

Across America, a quiet inefficiency plays out in millions of homes: large houses with many bedrooms occupied by small households. This isn't just about individual choice—it's a geographic pattern with profound implications for housing markets, family formation, and economic opportunity.

### Key Findings

- Systematic identification of housing inefficiency patterns by geography
- Empty nester concentrations in suburban and exurban areas
- Economic barriers to housing right-sizing revealed through spatial analysis
