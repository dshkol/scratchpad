## The Hidden Geography of Development Resistance

NIMBY (Not In My Backyard) attitudes shape American communities in profound ways, but they're difficult to measure directly. This analysis develops methods to detect NIMBY patterns using demographic and housing characteristics, revealing the geographic signature of development resistance.

[View full analysis →](/census-monkey-typewriter/analyses/geographic-nimby-detection.html)

### Key Findings

- Demographic and housing indicators that predict development resistance
- Geographic clustering of NIMBY-prone communities
- Relationship between housing values, demographics, and opposition to development

### Data Sources

- US Census Bureau American Community Survey
- Housing characteristics and demographic data
- Development pattern indicators
