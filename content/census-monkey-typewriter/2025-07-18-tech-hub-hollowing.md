## The Hollow Centers of Innovation

Tech hubs are supposed to be magnets for young talent and economic growth, but they often exhibit a paradoxical demographic pattern: hollowing out. This analysis explores how innovation centers create unexpected demographic dynamics that can undermine their own success.

[View full analysis →](/census-monkey-typewriter/analyses/tech-hub-hollowing.html)

### Key Findings

- Demographic patterns in major tech hubs reveal unexpected hollowing effects
- Housing costs and displacement create demographic distortions
- Innovation centers show distinct population composition patterns
