## The Annual Heartbeat of American Demographics

American population isn't static—it pulses with seasonal rhythms that reveal hidden patterns of migration, tourism, and economic activity. Some counties experience dramatic demographic swings that fundamentally alter their character throughout the year.

### Key Findings

- Extreme seasonal volatility counties identified with 300%+ population swings
- College towns, resort destinations, and agricultural regions show distinct patterns
- Seasonal demographics create unique economic and social dynamics
