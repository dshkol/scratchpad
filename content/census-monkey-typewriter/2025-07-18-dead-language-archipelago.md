## The Vanishing Voices of America

Heritage languages are disappearing across American communities, creating "dead language archipelagos"—regions where linguistic diversity has been systematically eroded. This analysis maps these patterns of language loss and explores their implications for cultural preservation.

### Key Findings

- Geographic patterns of heritage language decline mapped
- Identification of linguistic diversity hotspots and dead zones
- Relationship between language loss and demographic change

- Multi-decade trend analysis
