## The Hidden Geography of Digital Inequality

Broadband access has become essential infrastructure for economic opportunity, but its distribution across American communities reveals stark inequalities. This analysis maps the "infrastructure inequality gradient" and explores how digital divides shape economic outcomes.

[View full analysis →](/census-monkey-typewriter/analyses/infrastructure-inequality-gradient.html)

### Key Findings

- Geographic clustering of broadband deserts and digital opportunity zones
- Relationship between infrastructure access and economic mobility
- Rural-urban digital divides with surprising exceptions

### Data Sources

- US Census Bureau American Community Survey
- Broadband access and subscription data
- Federal Communications Commission infrastructure data
