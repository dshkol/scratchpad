## The Economic Power of Extended Family

Multi-generational households are often viewed as a sign of economic distress, but the data reveals a more complex story. In many communities, extended family living arrangements create significant economic advantages—a "grandparent dividend" that affects housing, childcare, and wealth accumulation.
