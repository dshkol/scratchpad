## The Last Stand of Large Families

In an era of declining fertility, pockets of America maintain fertility rates that seem to defy demographic trends. These "fertility frontiers" offer insights into the geographic, cultural, and economic factors that sustain large families.

[View full analysis →](/census-monkey-typewriter/analyses/fertility-frontier.html)

### Key Findings

- Geographic clustering of high-fertility communities identified
- Rural-urban fertility divides with surprising exceptions
- Cultural and economic factors that sustain large family formation

- US Census Bureau American Community Survey
- Household composition and family size data
- Rural-urban classification codes
