This collection showcases analyses from the [Census Monkey Typewriter project](/post/census-monkey-typewriter/) - an experiment in using large language models as research partners for demographic analysis.

Each analysis represents a complete research workflow: from hypothesis generation through data collection, statistical modeling, visualization, and narrative construction. The system explored serious demographic questions, tested novel methodological approaches, and occasionally ventured into whimsical territory.
