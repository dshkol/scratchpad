This analysis examines the paradoxical demographic effects of technology boom cycles, where innovation centers experience unexpected population and housing dynamics that can undermine their own economic foundations.

## Key Findings

- Tech hubs experience demographic "hollowing" as success drives out diverse populations
- Housing cost increases create systematic population turnover
- Worker composition shifts toward high-income technical roles, reducing economic diversity
- Geographic patterns reveal concentration of tech employment alongside demographic homogenization

## Methodology

The analysis uses American Community Survey data to track changes in:
- Occupational composition in major metropolitan areas
- Housing cost burdens and affordability patterns  
- Population flows and demographic turnover
- Economic diversity measures across tech-concentrated regions

**Data Sources:** US Census Bureau American Community Survey (2015-2021)  
**Geographic Level:** Metropolitan Statistical Areas (MSAs)  
**Methods:** Longitudinal demographic analysis, housing burden calculations, occupational diversity metrics
